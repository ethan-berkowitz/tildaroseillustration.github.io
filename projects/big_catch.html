the big catch

"you will not catch anything in there." she shouted at the boy from the top of the tree branch.

"did you hear what i said?" she asked. "yes," he said quietly, "i did."

then she sat down with him and watched the bobble. they watched it for a very long time, until it was dark.

suddenly, the bobble disappeared under water with great force, and the boy wrestled with it until a big fish flew into the air. it lit up the entire forest in its glow.
